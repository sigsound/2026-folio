During my time at Matterport, some of my favorite work has been in the creation and evolution of 3D tools for interior spaces. In addition to WebGL work, I was the Director of Product Design in charge of the mobile native apps and the matterport.com/discover experience.

Each one of these tools were individual projects with separate goals and objectives, user experience research, engineering collaboration, product and design thinking, and quantitative study. These tools allow our customers to measure, blur, trim, collaborate, and navigate their 3D spaces.

During my time here I also helped scale the design team from 3 to 20 people, including building out my own team of 5 high performing future design leaders.
