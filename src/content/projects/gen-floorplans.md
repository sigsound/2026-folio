During my time at Matterport, we tried to get automatic measurements off the ground as a project. Matterport had a floor plan add-on, which would kick off an ordering process to get a human operator to draw up a floor plan for you based on your LiDAR capture — totally manual. Very soon after I joined Polycam, we were able to achieve an automatic, professionally usable, 3D and 2D floor plan asset, along with automatic measurements. My experience directly led Polycam to a better product that, for a time, was the envy of the entire industry.

### Problem

Texture mesh reconstruction from iPhone LiDAR data is lower accuracy than professional cameras, and before Apple's RoomPlan API, Polycam did not have predictable straight surfaces for wall representations that could be used for instant measurement data. Apple's API was a short cut for us before we were able to create a custom solution. 

AEC professionals needed floor plans they could actually use professionally, and floor plan creation was either expensive, slow (manual labor services), or cheap & inaccurate. No tool produced a client-ready asset directly from a scan.

At Matterport, we saw the cost of floor plans regularly racing to the bottom, with cheaper and cheaper options relying on the labor. Customer expectations on speed grew faster and faster. For years, it's been clear that automation is the future for this asset.

### Constraints

LiDAR technology on iOS devices, which produce less accuracy than expensive professional cameras. During this period, we were also limited by Apple's RoomPlan API, which was a black box, with further development and improvement unclear. Another constraint was the size of our team — tiny. This project began with myself and one talented computer vision engineer. Limited resources also pushed us to release value faster to customers, whether the product was completely polished or not.

### Move

Start with incorporating thew new API, and get it working on the capture side — release early and often. Iterate on the feature over time to increase value while we more from customers. 

### Outcome

During the span of this project, we also introduced an Enterprise tier, a Business tier, and recognized the value difference between them and our small business customers. We started this work with barely useful outputs, and in the end, we completed a core workflow to compete with larger players in the space, like Magic Plan and Matterport. This work directly led to a certain company reaching out for acquisition talks very early in our development.
