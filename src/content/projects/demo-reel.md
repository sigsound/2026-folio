Featured motion and 3D work for clients including Adidas, Xbox, Intel, Samsung, Visa, Skullcandy, and Mideast Youth.

Motion design has been core to my practice since the beginning—from album covers and show posters to broadcast-quality brand work. C4D and After Effects have been my primary tools, with a growing interest in real-time 3D and generative methods.
