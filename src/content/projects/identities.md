Brand design has permeated the work of nearly every design position I've held. Here is a collection of that work, with examples of how brand identity often extends into motion and digital experiences.

BrainXchange is a small business founded to promote Interactive Learning and Intelligent Networking. With their logo, I created a full brand book, designed their first web experiences, and produced several print pieces. Other clients in this collection span technology, retail, hospitality, and professional services.
