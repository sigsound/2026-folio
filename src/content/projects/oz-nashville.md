OZ Arts supports the creation, development, and presentation of significant performing and visual artworks by leading artists whose contribution influences the advancement of their field. OZ worked with Aaron Rayburn for their identity, and with Fuzz for their site design and development.

OZ's site needed to work well with Eventbrite, big imagery, video, and audio. We built a parallax page featuring the various venue rooms OZ has to offer. The integration of live event data with rich editorial content created a seamless experience for both first-time visitors and loyal patrons.
