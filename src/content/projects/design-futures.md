Design Futures is a design-led annual trend methodology. It aims to embrace outside-in thinking to provide food for thought and to inform how we develop new designed products, services, and business models that will resonate with the changing world. The intent is to expose Intuit's community to content beyond Financial Services and our direct competitors—across the dimensions of Design, Technology, Brand, and Socioeconomic.

In this first year of the program at Intuit, I helped craft a cohesive visual style for all event materials and presentations, and assisted with strategy. I also worked with a developer to create an internal site, and printed large format posters for campus to promote the kickoff event.
