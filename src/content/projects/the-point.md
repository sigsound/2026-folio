The Point is a print and digital magazine of philosophical writing that embodies two distinct but complementary convictions: that humanistic thinking has relevance for contemporary life, and that our lives are full of experiences worth thinking about.

This was a freelance project for Paper Tiger. One of the elements that brought me joy was creating a different type of blog UI that let the reader explore categories and articles at a high level before diving in to read the full article—a more exploratory, browse-first reading experience.
