Your ultimate personal trainer. Fully loaded with 100+ workouts, clear audio and visual guidance for every move, plus collections to help you reach a range of goals with curated workout recommendations and expert tips.

In 2015 I helped the Nike+ team launch their first major update to the Nike Training Club app. I was one of many designers who contributed to this experience—working on the onboarding flow, landing screen design, iconography explorations, and motion studies for RPE feedback.

One of the key challenges was collaborating across timelines and across teams. We worked with AKQA, Instrument, and an internal team of about 15–20 designers. We also worked closely with engineering to ensure correct asset handoffs and created specs and requirements for content.
