Qualcomm came to Digital Trends with a request for a custom infographic site that would help explain how their modem technologies affect cell phone users. I used the analogy of traffic and package delivery to explain how important Snapdragon modems are to everyday uploads, Snapchat use, and more.

The illustrations were all designed to fit Snapdragon's style guide and current homepage design. I animated everything with Lottie, which allowed me to export directly from After Effects to code. It saved significant development time, and the client was thrilled with the result.
