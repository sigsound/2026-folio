During my time at Polycam, I designed every part of the application. In the early days, my main area was our cross-platform 3D tools team, which we dubbed "Polykit." The system is composed of a mixture of native iOS/Android and React Native for the 2D interface, and Google Filament for 3D, which allowed us to achieve a solid level of consistency across all three platforms.

Each one of these tools is a separate case study with goals informed by the needs of the business and the customers.
